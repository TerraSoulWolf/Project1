Where did we take her?

An Unlikely Adventurer

Canela saw a generous amount of British Columbia.

She adjusted very nicely to boat life, coming with us into Desolation Sound and the Gulf Islands! I distinctly remember her always sticking her nose out and smelling that salty air.

Road trips were in her agenda as well, whether it was over on Vancouver Island, or up to the Okanagan, she went where we went.

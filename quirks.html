Furry Personality

A Pupper Like No Other

Canela's personality began to take shape as soon as she got comfortable with our space.

In her early days, she slept in her crate; however it only took a few years for her to climb the comfort ladder, and sleep right in my dad's bed.

She was never really interested in chasing balls or sticks. Intead, she was obsessed with stuffed squeaky toys. She would shake them like mad, likely a killing reflex inherited from her Terrier genes

Like many dogs, she did have separation anxiety, especially directed towards my dad. Whenever he was gone, she would spend the time waiting in our foyer for him to return.

Having three legs never slower her down, her one back leg became quite strong, and compensated nicely. People seeing her for the first time would often not even notice she was missing one.

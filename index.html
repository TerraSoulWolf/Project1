Our Dog Canela
2012 - July 2026

A Quick Overview

After adopting her at the age of two, Canela was my family's lovely canine companion for twelve joyous years!


Quick Facts

  1. She was a rescue from Mexico.
  2. Canela means 'Cinnamon' in Spanish.
  3. She was a tripod, only 3 legs!
  4. We suspect she was a Poodle and Wheaten Terrier mix.
